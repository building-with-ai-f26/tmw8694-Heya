# Design System: Heya

Two systems live in this file, scoped by surface:

- **The Heya app look** (the eight canonical sections below, and every unprefixed token in the frontmatter) is Heya's design system. It is ported from the native SwiftUI app (`ios-native/Heya.swiftpm`, `Components.swift` and `Models.swift`) and shipped on the product page (`docs/index.html`). Every new surface starts here.
- **Sidewalk Chalk** (the last section, and every `wrapped-` token) describes the Wrapped web app (`app/index.html`) as it is built today, until it is restyled to the app look. Do not extend chalk to any other surface.

## Overview

**Creative North Star: "The Band, Lit"**

Heya's screens look like the iPhone app: a pure black ground, near-black rounded cards that sit on it without borders or shadows, white text at bold weights, and quiet gray for everything secondary. The surface stays dark and calm so the one thing that glows is the band. The 12-LED brushed-metal ring is the product's light, and on a surface that shows it, it is the hero. Color does not appear anywhere else unless it means a mood.

Density is the app's: generous black space around a single reading column, cards for anything that is a unit (a person, a poll, a table, a notice), and pill controls a thumb could hit. People are drawn as 12x12 one-bit pixel avatars on black tiles, never photos or illustrations. Hierarchy comes from size and weight in the system face (SF Pro on Apple devices, a self-hosted Inter elsewhere), not from extra faces, ornaments or color.

The app itself is dark-only. The web is not allowed to be: the project requires light mode, so the system carries an iOS grouped-gray light version (light ground, white cards, black text and actions) with the same structure and the same mood colors.

**Key Characteristics:**
- Pure black ground (iOS grouped gray in light mode), #171717-style cards at a 22px radius, no shadows on cards.
- One system face (SF Pro, Inter fallback) at bold display weights; quiet gray secondary text.
- The 12-LED brushed-metal ring with blurred LED glow is the signature object and the only light source.
- Mood colors only where a mood is meant: the selected mood pill, mood dots and ring LEDs, always next to the mood's name.
- Neutral actions are a solid white pill in dark mode and a solid black pill in light mode.
- 12x12 one-bit pixel avatars on black tiles stand for people.
- Small uppercase tracked labels name content; they are never kickers above headings.

## Colors

A neutral black-and-gray world with four mood colors that carry meaning and nothing else.

### Primary
- **Let's Hang Green** (hang): the "Let's hang" mood. The only green in the system.
- **Quiet Company Lilac** (quiet-mood): the "Quiet company" mood.
- **Busy-But-Wave Marigold** (wave): the "Busy, but wave" mood.
- **Off Gray** (off, off-light in light mode): the "Off" mood: an unlit LED, an Off dot, the selected Off pill.
- **Mood Ink** (on-mood): black text on a selected mood pill, in both schemes.

### Secondary
- **Neutral Action** (action / on-action; action-light / on-action-light): the solid fill for neutral actions and non-mood selected pills. White with black text on dark, black with white text on light. It is the text color promoted to a fill, so actions never borrow a mood.

### Neutral
- **Black Ground** (ground) / **Grouped Gray Ground** (ground-light): the page. Flat; no texture, no gradient.
- **Card** (card) / **White Card** (card-light): cards, notices, table wraps, pills at rest, the backing of screenshots. Matches the app's `Palette.card` (white 0.09).
- **Raised Card** (card-raised / card-raised-light): a control or code chip sitting on a card (poll pills, inline code).
- **Hairline** (line / line-light): 1px rules: pill outlines, table rows, the footer rule, the avatar tile edge in dark mode. Matches `Palette.cardBorder`.
- **Text** (text / text-light): headings, body, links.
- **Quiet** (quiet / quiet-light): secondary text, meta lines, labels, captions, list markers, link underlines. Matches `Palette.quiet` (white 0.64); the light value is darkened to hold contrast on gray and white.
- **Brushed Metal** (metal-highlight, metal-mid, metal-sheen, metal-shade, ring-groove): the ring's diagonal metal stops and its dark LED channel. Used only to draw the band.
- **Avatar Tile and Pixel** (avatar-tile, avatar-pixel): black tile, white pixels, in both color schemes.

### Named Rules
**The Mood Color Rule.** Green, lilac, marigold and Off gray mean Let's hang, Quiet company, Busy but wave, and Off. They are always shown with the mood's name in text, and the only mood-color fills are the selected mood pill, mood dots and ring LEDs. No mood color on headings, links, labels, borders, backgrounds or icons.

**The Neutral Action Rule.** A neutral action or a selected non-mood option fills with the text color (white on dark, black on light), never with a mood color.

**The Light Mode Is Required Rule.** Every surface ships both schemes. Light mode swaps grounds, cards, lines, text and actions; mood colors, the metal, and the black avatar tiles stay the same.

## Typography

**Display Font:** SF Pro Display via `-apple-system` (with self-hosted "Heya Inter", then Segoe UI, Roboto, Helvetica Neue, Arial)
**Body Font:** SF Pro Text via `-apple-system` (same fallbacks)
**Label/Mono Font:** ui-monospace / SF Mono for inline code only

**Character:** The iPhone's own voice: tight, heavy SF Pro headlines over relaxed SF Pro text, with nothing decorative. Inter (SIL OFL, `docs/fonts/`) stands in on Windows and Android; Apple devices never download it.

### Hierarchy
- **Display** (800, clamp(3.5rem, 9vw, 5.5rem), 0.95, -0.035em): the "Heya" wordmark heading only.
- **Headline** (800, clamp(1.75rem, 4vw, 2.1rem), 1.15, -0.02em): section headings, balanced wrapping. Inside a card it drops to 1.45rem.
- **Title** (700, 1.2rem, -0.01em): subsection headings; a person's name beside an avatar at 1.3rem.
- **Tagline** (500, 1.3rem, 1.4): the one-line product statement under the wordmark, max 26ch.
- **Body** (400, 17px, 1.55): reading text, pretty wrapping, in a 42rem measure.
- **Meta** (400, 0.9rem): quiet meta lines, figure captions, footer. Notices use 0.875rem.
- **Label** (600, 0.75rem, 0.09em, uppercase, quiet): section labels that name content (a table column, "Why him", "Before", a person's user group). Matches the app's `SectionLabel` (caption semibold, 1.2 tracking).
- **Control** (700, 1rem): pills. **Action** (700, 1.05rem): the action pill.

### Named Rules
**The One Face Rule.** The system face does everything. Hierarchy is size and weight; no second display face, no italic display, no decorative type.

**The Label Names Content Rule.** An uppercase tracked label is the name of the thing it sits on or beside (a table header, a card subsection, a person's group). It never floats above a heading as a kicker or eyebrow.

## Layout

One centered column, max 62rem, with 20px side gutters; text blocks inside it are held to a 42rem measure. The masthead is a two-column grid (1.1fr copy, 1fr band) with a 3rem gap and 4.5rem top padding: wordmark, tagline, meta and action pill on the left; the ring (about 260px), its mood readout and a four-pill mood pad (max 22rem) on the right. Sections open with 4.5rem above their headline. Cards stack with 1rem between them; figures take 1.75rem.

Below 760px the masthead stacks (copy, then the ring at about 210px, then a full-width pad). Below 560px tables in cards reflow into stacked rows, each value preceded by its column label. Spacing steps in use are 0.5, 0.75, 1, 1.75, 3 and 4.5rem.

## Elevation & Depth

Flat, by tonal layering: ground, then card, then raised card. Cards and controls carry no shadow and no border (pills have a 1px hairline). The only depth effects belong to the band. The LED glow is a Gaussian blur behind each LED core: it is the product's light, not decoration, and it stays at full strength in dark mode and 75% in light. In light mode the ring alone gets a soft drop shadow so the metal sits on a pale ground.

### Shadow Vocabulary
- **LED glow** (`feGaussianBlur stdDeviation 10` on a 13-unit circle behind a 4.1-unit core): every lit LED, in its mood color.
- **Ring on light** (`filter: drop-shadow(0 14px 22px rgba(0,0,0,.18))`): the ring in light mode only; transparent in dark.

### Named Rules
**The Only Light Rule.** The band is the only thing that glows. No glows, gradients or shadows on cards, text or controls; the metal gradient exists only on the ring.

## Shapes

Soft iOS rounded rectangles. Cards, table wraps and screenshots take a 22px radius (the app's `Card`); notices 16px; pills and focus rings 14px; inline code 6px. The action button is a full capsule (999px). Mood dots are circles. Avatar tiles are squares with corners of 2.5 pixel-units, and their pixels are drawn with crisp edges, never smoothed. The ring is a perfect circle: a thick brushed-metal band (stroke 17% of the diameter) with a dark channel down its middle holding 12 evenly spaced LEDs.

## Components

### Buttons
Solid and certain, like an iOS primary action.
- **Shape:** full capsule (999px), min height 50px, padding 0 1.6rem.
- **Primary:** neutral action fill with on-action text, action type.
- **Hover / Focus:** lifts 1px and fades to 0.88 opacity over 0.2s on cubic-bezier(.16, 1, .3, 1); presses to scale 0.98. Focus is a 3px solid outline in the text color, 3px offset, following the capsule.

### Chips (pills)
- **Style:** card fill, 1px hairline, 14px radius, min height 46px, padding 0 1rem, control type. On a card they sit on raised card with no hairline.
- **State:** hover brightens the hairline to quiet. Pressed (`aria-pressed`) fills with the neutral action color, or, in the mood pad only, with that mood's color and mood-ink text (Off fills with Off gray). Presses scale to 0.97. Colors cross-fade over 0.25s.

### Cards / Containers
- **Corner Style:** 22px.
- **Background:** card (white card in light mode).
- **Shadow Strategy:** none (see Elevation & Depth).
- **Border:** none.
- **Internal Padding:** 1.25rem 1.4rem; a poll card 1.4rem 1.5rem; a table card 0.4rem 1.1rem with hairline row rules.
- **Notice:** a quieter card (16px, 0.9rem 1.1rem, quiet text with the lead phrase in text color) for the tester notice.

### Navigation
The product page has no nav bar. In-page anchors are plain underlined links: text color, a 1px quiet underline offset 3px that turns text-colored on hover.

### The Ring (signature)
The 12-LED brushed-metal band, ported from the app's `RingView`. Each LED is a blurred glow plus a solid core in the current mood color. Changing mood relights it with the app's chase (each LED fades on over 0.55s, 45ms after the previous one); Quiet company breathes (opacity 1 to 0.28 and back over 1.85s); Off turns every LED out. Under reduced motion it changes instantly and does not breathe. A readout under it always names the mood beside its dot, with a quiet one-line meaning.

### Pixel Avatars (signature)
A person is a 12x12 one-bit pixel face (white pixels) on a black tile, 56px on the web, ported from `PixelAvatarView` and `AvatarTile`. The tile stays black in both color schemes; in dark mode it gets a hairline edge so it separates from the black ground. A name in title type and a section label sit beside it.

### Mood Dot
A 0.7rem circle in a mood color, always immediately followed by the mood's name.

## Do's and Don'ts

### Do:
- **Do** use mood colors only for the selected mood pill, mood dots and ring LEDs, and always with the mood's name.
- **Do** fill neutral actions and selected non-mood pills with the text color (white on dark, black on light).
- **Do** keep the ground flat black (iOS grouped gray in light mode) and put units of content on 22px cards with no shadow.
- **Do** ship light mode and dark mode on every surface, even though the iOS app is dark-only.
- **Do** let the ring's LED glow be the only glow on the page.
- **Do** use SF Pro (Heya Inter fallback) for everything, with hierarchy by size and weight.
- **Do** use uppercase tracked labels (0.75rem, 600, 0.09em, quiet) to name content: table columns, card subsections, a person's group.
- **Do** draw people as 12x12 one-bit pixel avatars on black tiles.
- **Do** honor prefers-reduced-motion: no chase, no breathing, no transitions.

### Don't:
- **Don't** use green, lilac, marigold or Off gray on headings, links, labels, borders, backgrounds or as decoration.
- **Don't** put an uppercase label above a heading as a kicker or eyebrow.
- **Don't** add shadows, glows or gradients to cards, text or controls; the metal gradient and LED glow belong to the band alone.
- **Don't** add a second typeface or a decorative display face.
- **Don't** use photos or illustrated characters for people; use pixel avatars.
- **Don't** carry Sidewalk Chalk (rough strokes, chalk filters, pavement texture, Bricolage Grotesque) onto any surface other than the Wrapped app.

## Wrapped App: Sidewalk Chalk (app/index.html)

**Scope.** This section and every `wrapped-` token describe the Wrapped web app (`app/index.html`) as built today. It stays on this system until it is restyled to the app look; then this section is retired. Nothing here applies to the product page, the prototype, or new surfaces. The Mood Color Rule above applies here too.

**Creative North Star: "Sidewalk Chalk"**

Your year of hangouts is drawn in chalk on the ground where it happened. The ground is pavement: sunlit pale concrete in light mode, charcoal asphalt with stone speckle in dark. Everything that is drawn (outlines, tallies, circled names, arrows, the year band, card fills, the hopscotch) is chalk, rendered as rough SVG strokes passed through turbulence filters so edges wobble and pavement pits show through. Nothing is a clean vector box, nothing glows, and nothing is a gradient.

The page is paged like a story, but the story is a hopscotch: numbered chalk squares on the left, the current one scrubbed in with chalk, and the current card at phone scale beside it. Each card is a chalked rectangle committed to one chalk color, and that color carries a mood when the card is about a mood. Hierarchy comes from scale in one display face. Lines draw themselves in when a card arrives and are already drawn under reduced motion. The world refuses full-bleed gradient story slides with giant neon numbers.

### Wrapped Colors
- **Mood chalks** (wrapped-chalk-green, wrapped-chalk-purple, wrapped-chalk-yellow): Let's hang, Quiet company, Busy but wave. A slab, hop square, swatch, map marker or mood bubble takes a mood chalk only when the data under it is that mood; otherwise white chalk. Always paired with the mood's name.
- **Leaf Ink** (wrapped-green-ink): a darker green legible on light concrete; resolves back to chalk green in dark mode. Used only for "Heya" in the Wrapped wordmark.
- **Grounds** (wrapped-concrete light, wrapped-asphalt dark), always under the SVG aggregate texture. Asphalt is also the share image's ground.
- **Chalk neutrals:** wrapped-graphite (light text, pressed button fill), wrapped-graphite-soft (secondary text), wrapped-line-graphite (drawn lines), wrapped-chalk-white (no-mood slabs, map popups), wrapped-on-chalk (text on any slab), and the dark swaps wrapped-night-chalk, wrapped-night-chalk-soft, wrapped-line-night.

**The Same-Chalk Swatch Rule.** A mood swatch on a slab of its own mood is scribbled in slab ink so it never disappears.

**The Ground Is Pavement Rule.** The Wrapped background is always ground color plus aggregate texture; never a flat fill, a gradient, or a white page.

### Wrapped Typography
Bricolage Grotesque (self-hosted variable woff2, 200 to 800) does all chalked lettering, roughened by a displacement filter; the system-ui stack handles controls and body. Roles: wrapped-display (wordmark, heavy chalk filter), wrapped-headline (the big number or name on each card; the title card goes up to clamp(2.8rem, 8vw, 4rem)), wrapped-title (card headlines, lite filter), wrapped-title-heavy (cover heading; side headings at 1.35rem), wrapped-statement (cover sentence, max 24ch, true numbers inline at 800 and 1.55em), wrapped-body (card copy at 1.05rem, max 34ch), wrapped-label (annotations, mood tags), wrapped-control (chalk buttons; "Make my Wrapped" uses the display face at 800, 1.3rem), wrapped-caption (card footers, run details, tabular numerals).

**The Chalk Lettering Rule.** Display and headline type carry the heavier chalk-text filter; titles, side headings and captions in the display face carry the lite filter. System-UI text is never filtered.

### Wrapped Layout
A centered column, max 70rem, padded 2rem 1.25rem 4rem. The story grid is hopscotch column | card (up to 27rem, phone scale, min 33rem tall) | side panel, 2rem gap. Below 62rem the side panel drops under; below 40rem everything stacks and the hopscotch becomes a row of six 48px squares. On wide screens the hopscotch is 66px squares in two columns with an 8px gap, numbered bottom to top.

### Wrapped Depth and Shapes
Flat: chalk lies on the ground, never casts a shadow and never floats. The one shadow (`0 10px 24px -8px rgb(0 0 0 / .45)`) is under the share-image preview, a printed photo lying on the pavement at -1.5deg. Every box is a seeded `roughRect` path (jittered corners, wobbly curves, overshooting stroke, 2.6 to 3.2px, round caps, `chalk-line` filter); circles are `roughEllipse` loops. Mood slabs have no outline; their edge is the ends of diagonal scrub strokes. The only CSS radii are the 6px focus ring and the 4px map tile clip.

### Wrapped Components
- **Chalk button:** rough chalk outline, transparent, graphite text, 46px. Pressed and primary fill with a graphite scrub and ground-colored text. Go button: display face 800 at 1.3rem, 62px. Hover rotates -1deg and lifts 1px over 0.25s on cubic-bezier(.16, 1, .3, 1). Focus: 3px dashed outline, 5px offset, 6px radius. Disabled: 45% opacity.
- **Card slab:** a two-pass chalk scrub (dense bed under copy, loose diagonal strokes on top) in one chalk; padding 2rem 1.9rem 1.6rem. Cover card: no fill, a 3.2px rough outline, tallies and the year band. Arrival: settles from 6px down and 0.6deg over 0.45s while lines draw in over 0.8s.
- **Hopscotch:** 66px chalk squares (48px on phones), display numerals at 800; the current square is scrubbed in its card's chalk; visited squares get a chalk tick; arrow keys and Back/Next do the same.
- **Chalk figures:** tallies (4 + diagonal, 3.5px), year band (month strokes, busiest month 9px vs 5.5px), circled top friend, mood swatches and bubbles (always beside the mood name), per-friend lanes (7px).
- **Map (adult only):** rough chalk frame around a 290px greyscaled Leaflet map (inverted in dark), mood-filled place circles sized by taps, with a named mood key.
- **Share image:** 1080x1350 PNG on night asphalt, a white-chalk hopscotch of the year with mood scrubs only where a mood applies.

### Wrapped Do's and Don'ts
- **Do** draw every outline and figure as a seeded rough path through the chalk-line filter.
- **Do** commit each card to exactly one chalk: its mood's if it is about a mood, white otherwise.
- **Do** let chalk lines draw in on arrival (0.8s) and render them pre-drawn under prefers-reduced-motion.
- **Don't** use mood chalks decoratively, or anything that reads as Spotify Wrapped.
- **Don't** put CSS borders, radius boxes or drop shadows on chalk elements.
- **Don't** show locations, maps or sharing on a kid's Wrapped.
